Run experiments, not goals

This quarter I’m not setting goals. I’m running experiments, because an experiment is allowed to fail and a goal, somehow, never is.

I read that the reasons people are lazy come down to three: you can be a cynic about it, a perfectionist about it, or an escapist. They’re really just different spots on two scales, how much you want to achieve and how willing you are to do the work.

The star is where I’m trying to live: wanting something and actually doing something about it, with permission to fail.

The sweet spot is experimentalism, high on both. The trick is to call everything an experiment. If it’s an experiment, it’s okay to fail, there’s no pressure to succeed, and all I have to do is notice what worked and what didn’t and carry it into the next quarter. Quarterly experiments, not quarterly goals.
