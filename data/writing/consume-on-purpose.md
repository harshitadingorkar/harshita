---
title: Consume on purpose, and forgive the rest
date: 2026-01-06
summary: Take notes as I consume, mix in the mindless, and forgive the days I don’t.
---
Being influenced is a large part of the creative process, so I want to consume more, and more mindfully.

The method is simple: take notes while I consume. I saw a video about this, and it works. The things I take in feel better digested, even when they are things I would have loved anyway. Talking about what I’ve taken in does the same job.

It doesn’t have to be all productive. That wouldn’t be sustainable, and it isn’t the point. The mix can include mindless consumption. I only want to be a little intentional about what goes in.

Some days I won’t want to do any of it. On those days I’ll forgive myself and watch trash. The aim is to stay absorbent to my surroundings without force, shame or guilt, because that absorbency is where ideas come from.

One structural change supports this: I left Instagram, and I’m staying off it for a quarter. Then I’ll revisit and see what it did for me.
