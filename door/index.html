Working with the door open

Most of what gets shown online is the finished thing. This is the other part: what I’m working on right now, a line at a time, while it’s still half made and might not work at all.

I’m not announcing anything here. I’m just leaving the door open, so you can see the work as it happens.

The idea comes from Andy Matuschak’s note on working with the garage door up, which grew out of Robin Sloan walking past a woodworker who always keeps his shop door propped open. I am here, working.

    Nothing of this kind yet.
