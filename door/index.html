Working with the door open

This is where I leave the door propped open: the things I’m working on, a line or two at a time, while they’re still half made. Nothing here is finished. That’s the point.

The idea comes from Andy Matuschak’s note on working with the garage door up, which riffs on Robin Sloan and a woodworker who always keeps his door open. I am here, working.
