What this is

Things I keep: images, references and small artifacts I come back to. Gathered on are.na, arranged here by curation. All rights remain with the original creators.

Clay as meditation

Clay won’t be hurried. It dries when it dries, and it remembers every press of the thumb. Hand-building has no undo, only the next small decision. It’s the slowest thing I do, and the one that makes the rest make sense.

New pieces, and the ones that survive the kiln, go up on Instagram.
